Why Talofa

Care that begins with listening

Talofa means “hello” in Samoan. For us, it represents a genuine welcome: taking time to understand each person, their family, culture, goals and way of life.

  • Person-centred careYour preferences, choices and goals guide the support we provide.
  • Culturally respectfulWelcoming support for Pasifika people and families, and for people from every community.
  • Flexible and reliableClear communication and dependable support shaped around changing needs.

Let’s talk about the support that feels right for you

Whether you are an NDIS participant, family member, carer or support coordinator, we are ready to listen and help you understand the next step.
