About Talofa

A warm welcome. A genuine connection.

Good support starts with seeing the person, listening well and building trust over time.

Our story

Care shaped by family and community

Talofa Support Services grew from a belief that every person deserves to feel welcomed, respected and genuinely supported.

Talofa, the Samoan word for “hello”, reflects the warmth we bring to each connection. Our Pasifika identity informs our understanding of family and collective care, and we welcome people from every culture and community.
