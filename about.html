About Talofa

A warm welcome. A genuine connection.

We believe good support starts with seeing the person, listening well and building trust over time.

Our story

Care shaped by family and community

Talofa Support Services grew from a simple belief: every person deserves to feel welcomed, respected and genuinely supported. Inspired by the strength of family and community, our approach is personal, practical and grounded in relationships.

The name Talofa, the Samoan word for “hello”, reflects the spirit we bring to each connection. We aim to meet people with warmth and fa'aaloalo, or respect, while listening carefully to what matters to them. Our Pasifika identity informs our understanding of family and collective care, and we welcome people from all cultures and communities.
