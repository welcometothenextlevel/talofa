Aged care support partnerships

Dignity, connection and care at every age

Talofa provides aged care support on a subcontracting basis through partnerships with registered aged care providers.

How we work

Support delivered through trusted partnerships

We support older people under arrangements with registered aged care providers. This means the registered provider remains responsible for the care plan and service oversight, while Talofa may deliver agreed day-to-day support as their subcontractor.

Clear and honest: Talofa Support Services does not currently claim independent aged care registration. Aged care support is delivered in partnership with registered providers.

Our approach

Care that respects the whole person

Within our partnership arrangements, support is guided by the person’s care plan, preferences and provider requirements.

Respect and dignity

We approach personal routines, home life and conversations with patience, privacy and care.

Culture and connection

We recognise the importance of family, language, culture and community in a person’s wellbeing.

Consistent communication

We communicate openly with the registered provider and the person’s chosen support network.

Discuss an aged care partnership
