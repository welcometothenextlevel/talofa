Aged care partnership

Dignity, connection and care at home

Talofa provides agreed in-home support through our service partnership with Trilogy Care.

How the partnership works

Support with clear responsibilities

Trilogy Care supports clients with care planning, funding administration and service oversight. Talofa provides agreed day-to-day support in the home, following the client’s care plan and preferences.

This arrangement helps older people receive warm, dependable support while remaining connected to their chosen aged care provider and care-management team.

In partnership with Trilogy Care

What this means for clients

  • Your aged care plan and funding remain managed through Trilogy Care.
  • Talofa may deliver the agreed practical support services in your home.
  • Both organisations work within their defined roles and responsibilities.

Learn more about Trilogy Care

Clear and honest: Talofa Support Services does not claim independent aged care provider registration. Aged care services are delivered through the partnership arrangement described above.

Our approach

Care that respects the whole person

Support is guided by the care plan and the person’s preferences, culture, routines and chosen support network.
