Aged care partnership

Dignity, connection and care at home

Talofa provides agreed in-home support through our partnership with Trilogy Care.

How it works

Support with clear responsibilities

Trilogy Care supports clients with care planning, funding administration and service oversight. Talofa provides agreed day-to-day support in the home, following the care plan and preferences.

In partnership with Trilogy Care

  • Your plan and funding remain managed through Trilogy Care.
  • Talofa may deliver agreed practical support in your home.
  • Both organisations work within their defined roles.
Clear and honest: Talofa does not claim independent aged care provider registration. Aged care support is delivered through this partnership.
